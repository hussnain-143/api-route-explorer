Brand Rules & Best Practices

Ensuring visual precision, developer-grade consistency, and high brand recall.

DO (Brand Best Practices)
  • Use Emerald Green #10B981 and Teal #14B8A6 as the primary brand drivers.
  • Use Cyan #06B6D4 only as an intentional secondary accent.
  • Maintain high contrast between Deep Navy (#0B1220) and typography.
  • Keep the route highway and arrow horizontal and balanced.
DON'T (Strict Visual Prohibitions)
  • Do NOT use the typical purple/indigo developer-tool palette.
  • Do not add generic 3D effects, cartoon robots, or animal mascots.
  • Do not replace the symbol with generic server/cloud/database clipart.
  • Do not introduce rainbow gradients or cluttered drop-shadows.
